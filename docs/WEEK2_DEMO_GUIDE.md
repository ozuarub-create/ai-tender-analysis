1. Run `python demo_week2.py`.
2. Show the 90-pair benchmark and explain the five status rules.
3. Open `exports/week2_compliance_matrix.xlsx` and filter by bidder, category, status, and mandatory flag.
4. Show tender and bid source references behind selected findings.
5. Open the deviation register and highlight seeded exclusion, delivery, warranty, alternative proposal, and contradiction examples.
6. Open the clarification export and show bidder + requirement IDs.
7. Show `results/week2_evaluation_results.json` for exact agreement, per-status results, and confusion matrix.
8. Confirm the `reserved_week4` folder from Week 1 remains untouched.
